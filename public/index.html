Scope Guide

none: no permission.

user: records owned by the user or their teams.

business: records in the user's business unit.

parent: records in the user's business unit and child business units.

org: records across the organization.

recordfilter: special filtered access where Dataverse exposes it for a privilege.

Table files use Create, Read, Write, Delete, Append, Append To, Assign, and Share. XLSX files hide metadata columns and add dropdown validation for editable scopes.
